15 slides, designed for approximately 10 minutes including discussion.

## Coverage

The presentation explains misinformation, disinformation, deepfakes, impersonation and recommender systems. It examines three documented cases: the 2024 Biden robocalls in New Hampshire, the 2023 fabricated Slovak election audio, and the Meliorator AI-assisted bot farm disclosed in 2024.

The ethical analysis uses Laudon & Laudon’s Figure 4.1 at individual, societal and political/policy levels, including accountability and control. It evaluates technical, organisational and regulatory responses and discusses freedom of expression. Documented events are distinguished from uncertain effects on election outcomes.
